Privacy Policy

Kovazen and its divisions: Kovazen Interactive Studio, Kovazen Labs and Kovazen Digital · Last updated: 14 November 2026

This policy explains how Kovazen ("we", "us") handles information across our website, games, apps and services. Each product section below adds the details that apply to that product. We keep things simple: we collect as little as we can, and we never sell your personal information.

1. Who we are

Kovazen is a group made up of Kovazen Interactive Studio (games), Kovazen Labs (software products) and Kovazen Digital (digital marketing). Contact for all privacy questions: hello@kovazen.com.

2. Our website (kovazen.com)

  • The website does not use cookies, analytics trackers or advertising.
  • It is hosted on GitHub Pages and served through Cloudflare. Like any web host, they may record technical information such as your IP address and browser type to deliver and protect the site.
  • If you email us, we use your email address and message only to reply, and keep them only as long as needed.

3. Our games and apps

Each of our apps lists its own details below. As products launch, they are added here.

HERD (Android game by Kovazen Interactive Studio, package com.kovazen.herd)

No account needed. HERD has no login and does not ask for your name, email, contacts, photos or precise location.

Stored on your phone only. Your progress (levels, stars, coins, hats, settings) is saved on your device and is not sent to us. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes it.

Advertising (Google AdMob). HERD is free and shows ads through Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and prevent fraud, Google's Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and share:

  • Your device's IP address (which can indicate an approximate location)
  • Interactions with ads and the app (for example ad views and taps, app launches)
  • Diagnostic information (app and SDK performance)
  • Device identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID

This data is encrypted in transit. Where required by law (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland), the game asks for consent before personalised ads are shown. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalisation, in your Android settings. How Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

In-app purchases (Google Play). Payments are handled entirely by Google Play; we never see card, UPI or bank details. When you buy something, the game sends the product name and Google's purchase token to our verification service, which asks Google Play whether the purchase is genuine. This is used only for that check and is not stored by us.

Inviting friends. If you choose to invite a friend, the game uses your phone's own share screen so you can send an invite message through an app you pick (for example WhatsApp). The invite and reward codes are created on your device; the game does not read your contacts and does not send anything to our servers to do this.

Age. HERD is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13.

4. Business services (Kovazen Labs and Kovazen Digital)

When we provide software or marketing services to a business client, we handle any personal information only on that client's instructions and under our agreement with them. The client's own privacy policy explains how they use that information. Product-specific notices will be added here as our Labs products launch.

5. What we never do

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will remove it.

6. Your rights

You can ask us what personal information we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it, by emailing hello@kovazen.com. For data collected by Google for advertising, use your Android settings or Google's tools. Users in India may contact us under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023; users in the EEA/UK under the GDPR. We will respond within a reasonable time.

7. Security

We use encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and keep the information we hold to a minimum.

8. Changes

We will update this policy when our products change. The "Last updated" date above shows the latest version.

Privacy Policy

Kovazen Interactive Studio · Applies to the game HERD (Android, package com.kovazen.herd) · Last updated: 10 November 2026

This policy explains what information the HERD game uses, why, and the choices you have. We keep it simple: HERD has no accounts, no login and does not ask for your name, email, contacts, photos or precise location.

1. Information stored on your phone only

Your game progress (levels, stars, coins, hats, settings and similar) is saved on your own device. It is not sent to us. Uninstalling the game or clearing its app data deletes it.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

HERD is free and shows ads through Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google's Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and share:

  • Your device's IP address (which can indicate an approximate location)
  • Interactions with ads and the app (for example ad views and taps, app launches)
  • Diagnostic information (app and SDK performance)
  • Device identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID

This data is encrypted in transit. Where required by law (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland), the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalisation, in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads). See how Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

3. In-app purchases (Google Play)

Payments are handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your card, UPI or bank details. When you buy something, the game sends the purchase's product name and Google's purchase token to our verification service, which asks Google Play whether the purchase is genuine, so that we can deliver it and prevent fraud. This information is used only for that check and is not stored by us.

4. What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, contacts, photos, microphone or camera data, or precise location.
  • The game has no chat and no way to share information with other players.

5. Children

HERD is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

6. Your rights

Because we do not hold personal data about you on our own servers, there is usually nothing for us to access or delete; your game data lives on your device. For data collected by Google for ads, use the Android settings above or Google's tools. Indian users may contact us under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023; EEA/UK users under the GDPR. We will respond within a reasonable time.

7. Security

Data sent by the game is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). Save data on your device is protected against tampering.

8. Changes

We may update this policy when the game changes. The "Last updated" date above shows the latest version.
